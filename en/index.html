How it works

Four moves,
behind glass.

People stop and watch. It pulls footfall to your space.

  1. Two 24″ screens. Two customers order and pay at the same time. The bar queues and prepares in order.

  2. The cup is staged at payment, so preparation starts straight away.

  3. A Dobot Nova 5 arm moves the cup between the coffee, ice, milk and powder modules.

  4. The finished drink turns out to the customer. Faster pickup, smaller footprint.

Operations

The Cleaning App

Put the unit in cleaning mode and the app opens by itself. Each step is shown as an image and a short video. A step must be confirmed before the next one appears, so nothing gets skipped.

  • Daily cycle: 20 to 40 minutes
  • Weekly, monthly and three-monthly cycles
  • One day of training for cleaning staff
  • Every step is logged. Your site keeps a hygiene record for inspections.

Refilling

Who refills, how often, how long.

Your own team, as part of the daily routine, guided by the Cleaning App.

Fill levels for cups, milk and ingredients show in the fleet web app, so refills are planned, not reactive.

Questions

Which business models do you offer?

Three models. Revenue share, with no upfront investment. Revenue share with buy-in, for established locations that want a larger share. Leasing, a fixed monthly cost plus a small fee per drink, for sites that offer drinks free of charge. In every model SMYZE supplies the Robobarista, all ingredients, support, monitoring and maintenance. Exact terms are shared with partners.

Three models. Revenue share splits revenue 70/30 between SMYZE and the partner, with no upfront investment and a revenue benchmark of at least CHF 3,000 per month for SMYZE. Revenue share with buy-in splits revenue 50/50 after a one-time partner buy-in of CHF 40,000. Leasing costs CHF 4,000 per month plus CHF 0.80 per drink, and suits sites that offer drinks free of charge. In every model SMYZE supplies the Robobarista, all ingredients, support, monitoring and maintenance.

What daily volume does a site need?

About 60 to 80 drinks a day is the minimum viable volume for a Robobarista site.

How fast can we go live?

A Robobarista is live 4 to 6 weeks after signature.

How long does installation take?

The unit arrives in two halves and is assembled on site. Installation takes about 4 hours on site (target). You are live 4 to 6 weeks after signature.

Who refills and cleans it?

One trained person. The daily clean takes 20 to 40 minutes, guided step by step by the Cleaning App. Weekly, monthly and three-monthly cycles come on top. Training takes one day. Refilling is part of the same routine.

What happens if it breaks?

Every unit is monitored remotely by the SMYZE team, and any irregularity is flagged immediately to the SMYZE Remote Support Team. Errors also show up in the fleet app as tickets, and SMYZE supplies the spare parts.

Can it go outdoors?

Yes. One variant covers indoor and outdoor sites. Operating range: −10 °C to +40 °C. Outdoor installs include a roof.Yes. One variant covers indoor and outdoor sites. Operating range: 14 °F to 104 °F. Outdoor installs include a roof.

Can we put our own coffee in it?

Yes. We are flexible on the choice of coffee beans, in line with our quality standards.

Can we set our own prices?

Prices are agreed together and set according to market pricing, local competition and other factors that affect sales. SMYZE controls the entire software, so prices can be adjusted easily at any point.

Can we co-brand it?

Yes. Co-branding is possible, in line with our templates and standards.

Who owns the sales data, and can we access it?

Partners get dashboard access, reports and exports.

Is it available in the US?

Yes. V3 comes in EU and US versions on one platform, with native power for each market and no converters. Robobaristas are already live in Europe and the US.

Does it take cash?

No. The Robobarista is cashless. An Adyen terminal takes Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, Amex, Diners, Discover, JCB and V PAY.

What happens if it loses connection?

Connection drops don't stop the bar. Orders in progress finish, and data syncs when the signal is back. New card payments need a signal.
